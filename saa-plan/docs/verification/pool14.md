## Could not settle

- **q14-021:** no page says in words that Redshift local tables are faster than Spectrum. The rejection of "query everything through Spectrum" rests on the Athena FAQ sentence about ingesting into the warehouse "for high performance analytics".
- **q14-035 (MemoryDB):** the MemoryDB FAQ says "We recommend using ElastiCache for new workloads", and ElastiCache for Valkey 9.0 and later has durability. The key holds because the only ElastiCache options are Memcached and asynchronous Redis OSS; the explanation says so. MemoryDB is not on the exam guide's in-scope list.
- **q14-036 (Timestream):** Timestream and QLDB are not on the in-scope list either. No ledger question was written: QLDB is shut down and Amazon Managed Blockchain is in sunset.
- **q14-005:** "applications reach a Kinesis data stream through the Kinesis API, not as Kafka topics" is from the Kinesis introduction page; no page states the negative.
- **q14-009:** "AWS recommends the Glue Data Catalog" is the EMR release guide's wording for a persistent or shared metastore, not a general recommendation.
- **q14-016:** "survives a zone failure only with less capacity left" for two zones is reasoning from the Multi-AZ page, not a quoted sentence.
- Judgement statements ("custom code to operate", "more development") have no source page.
