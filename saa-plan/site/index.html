🗺 Where things happen: 4 separate places

AWS splits learning, the actual cloud, and exam booking into different sites with different logins. Every step says which one it uses.

① Skill Builder

skillbuilder.aws
  • The learning site you already use (login: AWS Builder ID).
  • Lecture courses, labs and the Exam Prep Plan.
  • Some of it is free. One month of subscription from Day 1 opens the rest; it is billed to ③ your AWS account.
  • It is not an AWS account — you can't build anything here.

② Lab sandbox

opened from inside a lab on Skill Builder
  • Each AWS SimuLearn or AWS Builder Lab opens a temporary AWS account that AWS gives you.
  • Real AWS console, wiped after the lab.
  • No card, no setup, nothing to clean up.

③ Your own AWS account

console.aws.amazon.com
  • A real, permanent cloud account you create on Day 1.
  • Needs a card and the Paid plan, because the subscription is billed here. Sign-up credits are used first; anything beyond them is charged.
  • Used for every Build step.

④ Certification account

aws.training → Certification
  • Only for booking and managing the exam.
  • Booking hands you over to Pearson VUE (the exam provider).
  • Online from home or at a test centre.

Rules: lectures first, then their labs, in the order of the plan. Watch lectures at 1.25–1.5×. Labs beat videos — if short on time, cut video, never labs. In ③ your own account, delete what you built every day. For every wrong practice answer, write why each wrong option is wrong. Dotted-underlined terms on a step show a definition on hover.

Course, module, lesson and lab names follow the outlines of the AWS Solutions Architect Learning Plan (Includes Labs) and the Exam Prep Plan (SAA-C03) as Skill Builder listed them on 12 October 2026. If a name is worded differently in your view, go by the order: lectures first, then their labs. Quiz and mock questions are original practice questions written in the exam's style, not official AWS questions. Progress is saved on the server when it runs from Docker, and in this browser either way; export a backup in ⚙ Settings now and then.
